# Ergonomic notes cited from the public standards

These are short notes for the later RAG corpus. Each number is taken from the named section. Retailer catalogs are not a source.

## Work surface and knee clearance (2010 ADA Standards)

Source: `datasets/raw/rag/2010-ada-design-standards.pdf`.

- A kitchen work surface is 34 inches (865 mm) maximum above the floor. An adjustable counter may vary from 29 inches (735 mm) to 36 inches (915 mm). Section 804.3.2.
- Knee clearance is the space under an element between 9 inches (230 mm) and 27 inches (685 mm) above the floor. Section 306.3.1.
- Where knee clearance is required, it is 11 inches (280 mm) deep minimum at 9 inches above the floor, and 8 inches (205 mm) deep minimum at 27 inches above the floor. Section 306.3.3.

## Seat, bed, reach, and storage (MoHUA 2021)

Source: Harmonised Guidelines and Standards for Universal Accessibility in India, 2021, section numbers below. Copyright 2021 Gaurav Raheja, IIT Roorkee, NIUA and MoHUA.

- Resting-seat height along long routes is 450 mm to 500 mm. Section 4.4.2.
- A bed for a wheelchair user is 450 mm to 480 mm from the floor. Section 6.1.6.
- Kitchen shelves and storage in the residential guidance are between 300 mm and 1200 mm from the floor. Section 6.1.5.
- Door hardware is mounted 800 mm to 1100 mm from the floor and is operable with one hand. Section 4.10.8.
