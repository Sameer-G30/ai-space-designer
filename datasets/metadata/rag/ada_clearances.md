# 2010 ADA Standards: clearance notes

Source: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design, Department of Justice, PDF `datasets/raw/rag/2010-ada-design-standards.pdf` (also https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/). These are short notes for retrieval. The PDF is the text to quote in a later phase.

- Walking surfaces are 36 inches (915 mm) minimum clear width, with a short 32 inch (815 mm) exception. Section 403.5.1.
- An accessible route narrower than 60 inches (1525 mm) needs a passing space at least every 200 feet (61 m). The passing space is 60 by 60 inches, or a T-shaped space under section 304.3.2. Section 403.5.3.
- A circular turning space is 60 inches (1525 mm) minimum diameter. Section 304.3.1.
- Clear floor space is 30 inches (760 mm) by 48 inches (1220 mm) minimum. Section 305.3.
- Door openings are 32 inches (815 mm) minimum clear width, measured with a swinging door open 90 degrees. Openings deeper than 24 inches (610 mm) are 36 inches (915 mm) minimum. Section 404.2.3.
- A pass-through kitchen with counters or appliances on opposing sides needs 40 inches (1015 mm) minimum between them, and two entries. Section 804.2.1.
- A U-shaped kitchen enclosed on three sides needs 60 inches (1525 mm) minimum between opposing cabinets, counters, appliances, or walls. Section 804.2.2.
- Knee clearance under an element is the space from 9 inches (230 mm) to 27 inches (685 mm) above the floor. Section 306.3.1. Where it is required, it is at least 11 inches (280 mm) deep at 9 inches above the floor and 8 inches (205 mm) deep at 27 inches above the floor. Section 306.3.3.
