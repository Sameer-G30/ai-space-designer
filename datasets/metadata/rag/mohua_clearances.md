# MoHUA Harmonised Guidelines 2021: clearance notes

Source: Harmonised Guidelines and Standards for Universal Accessibility in India, 2021, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, PDF `datasets/raw/rag/mohua-harmonised-guidelines-2021.pdf`. Copyright 2021 Gaurav Raheja, IIT Roorkee, NIUA and MoHUA. These are short notes for retrieval, not a copy of the book.

- One-way corridor clear width for a wheelchair is 1200 mm. Section 4.4.1.
- An accessible route where a wheelchair and a walking person pass is 1500 mm clear, except extra space at doorways. Section 4.4.1.
- Two wheelchairs passing need 1800 mm clear width. Section 4.4.1.
- Office work-area accessible routes and an accessible checkout lane are at least 900 mm. Section 4.4.1.
- A doorway clear opening is 900 mm, measured between the face of the door and the door stop with the door open 90 degrees. Section 4.10.2.
- A corridor needs at least 1200 mm to let a wheelchair make a 90 degree turn through a door. Section 4.10.7.
- Door handles, pulls, latches, and locks are mounted 800 mm to 1100 mm above the floor. Section 4.10.8.
- A bedroom provides 1500 mm turning space for a wheelchair, at least near the doors. Section 6.1.6.
